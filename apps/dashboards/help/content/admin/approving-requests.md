# Approving requests

When a parent registers a child, nothing happens until you say yes. Their
request sits and waits. This page is where you decide.

## Where to find it {#where}

Click **Requests** at the top of any admin page. The number next to it is your
to-do list: new requests plus families asking to leave a class.

![The Requests page, with pending requests listed in a table](requests.png)

## The rule to remember {#rule}

> **Approve when you are sure the class will go ahead.**
>
> Approving is not a note to yourself. The family gets an email within
> seconds saying their child has a place. If you then have to cancel the
> class, they have already told the child.

If you are still waiting to hear whether a provider is confirmed, or whether
enough children have signed up, leave the requests where they are. They keep
until you are ready. Nobody is waiting on a screen.

## Approving one child {#approve}

Each row shows when the family registered, the child, their guardians, the
class, and one thing worth reading before you click: the **Availability**
pill.

![One request row, showing the availability pill and the Approve and Reject buttons](request-row.png)

- **14 free** (green, with whatever number of places is left) — approving
  gives the child a place. They are enrolled, and the family gets the
  "Confirmed" email.
- **Full → waiting list** (amber) — the class has no room. Approving still
  works, but it puts the child on the waiting list instead, and the family
  gets an email saying so. This is the normal way a waiting list is built.

So the pill tells you what your click will do before you make it.

**Reject** turns the request down and emails the family. You are asked to
confirm first, because there is no undo.

If the child's age is outside the class's recommended range, an amber pill
says so. It is a flag for your judgement, not a block — you can still approve.

A blue **Your child** pill means the request is for one of your own children
(your admin account is also your family account — **My family** at the top of
the public site). You can decide on it like any other; the pill is there so a
colleague can take it instead if your team prefers that.

## Approving several at once {#bulk}

For a long queue, go to **Enrolments → Enrolments**, tick the rows you want,
and pick one of these from the **Action** menu:

- *Approve selected requests*
- *Reject selected requests*

You get a summary afterwards, for example: *Approved: 3 enrolled, 1 added to a
waiting list (class full), 1 skipped (class no longer open).*

## When approving does not work {#blocked}

You will see *"This class is no longer open (cancelled, archived or past its
term)."* if the class is still a draft, has been cancelled or archived, or its
term is no longer the active one. Fix the class first, then come back.

## Changing your mind {#undo}

There is no undo button, but nothing is stuck. Go to **Enrolments →
Enrolments**, find the row, and use the action *Cancel selected enrolments
(notifies families)*. The family is told — which is why it is better to be
sure first.

## Families asking to leave {#cancellations}

The lower half of the same page is **Cancellation requests**.

A parent can withdraw a child on their own for a short while after
registering — two weeks unless you change it, under **Accounts → Site
configuration → Registrations → Withdrawal window days**. After that, they can
only *ask*, and the request lands here. **The child keeps the place until you
decide.**

> If you change that number, change the terms and conditions too (Site
> configuration, at the bottom). They state the same period in words, and
> nothing keeps the two in step for you.

![The Cancellation requests section, with Confirm cancellation and Keep place buttons](cancellation-requests.png)

- **Confirm cancellation** — the place ends and the family is told. If anyone
  is on the waiting list for that class, you get an alert that a seat is free.
  See [Waiting lists and offers](waiting-lists).
- **Keep place** — the family is told you will be in touch. Use this when
  there is a conversation still to have.

The **Waiting list** column tells you what confirming would unlock: *2 waiting
for a seat*, or *Nobody waiting*.
