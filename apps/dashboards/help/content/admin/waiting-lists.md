# Waiting lists and offers

A waiting list is not a queue that empties itself. When a seat frees up, you
choose who gets it. This page explains how children get onto the list, and
what happens when you hand one of them a place.

## How a child ends up waiting {#how}

There is only one way: you **approve a request for a class that is full**.
Instead of a place, the child gets a spot on the waiting list, and the family
is emailed to say so. See [Approving requests](approving-requests).

A class with its capacity set to **0** is a waiting-list-only class. Parents
can register, and everyone waits until you raise the capacity.

## Where to look {#roster}

Open a class and click **Roster**, or click the roster link on the class list.

![The roster page, with the seat counts along the top](roster.png)

The pills along the top are the class in one line. Two of them look similar
and mean different things:

- **… seats taken (incl. outstanding offers)** and **… seats free to offer** —
  what *you* have to give away. Children already enrolled, plus offers still
  waiting for an answer.
- **Parents see … available** — what the *catalogue* shows. This also
  subtracts requests you have not reviewed yet and everyone on the waiting
  list, so families are not tempted by places that are already spoken for.

In the picture above there is one seat free to offer, and parents are shown
none available — because three families are already waiting for it. The two
numbers disagreeing is normal, not a bug.

## Handing out a freed seat {#offer}

When a place frees up — a family leaves, or you confirm a cancellation — the
admins looking after that class get an email, if anyone is waiting. Nothing
else happens automatically.

Go to the roster and scroll to **Waiting list**.

![The waiting list section of the roster, with an Offer seat button on each row](roster-waiting-list.png)

The list is in order of who registered first, and the page says so plainly:
*first-come order shown as guidance; you choose who gets a freed seat.* Use
your judgement — siblings, age, how long someone has waited.

Click **Offer seat** on the row you choose. The family gets an email inviting
them to take the place.

## The clock {#clock}

An offer does not last forever. The family has **48 hours** by default, and
the exact deadline is shown on the roster under **Outstanding offers**.

To change it: **Accounts → Site configuration → Registrations → Offer ttl
hours**.

While an offer is out, it holds the seat. That is why the offer shows in the
seat count — you cannot accidentally give the same place away twice.

Three things can happen:

- **The family confirms.** The child is enrolled and gets the usual
  confirmation email.
- **The family declines.** The place comes back to you and you get an alert.
- **Nobody answers in time.** The offer expires by itself, the family is told
  the place has gone, and you get an alert. The seat is free again straight
  away.

## Offering when the class is full {#over-capacity}

Sometimes you know one more will fit. The app lets you, on purpose, but it
makes you say so: when there are no free seats, the button reads **Offer seat
(over capacity)** and asks you to confirm, naming the numbers.

> Afterwards the roster shows **1 seat over capacity** in red, so the class
> never goes over quietly.

This is the only way a class ends up above its capacity. Approving a request
never does it.

## Who else can see the list {#others}

Providers see the waiting list for their own classes on their dashboard, and
the number waiting. They cannot offer seats — that is yours.

The **Download CSV** link on the roster gives you everyone in one file:
enrolled, offered, waiting and requested, with waiting-list positions filled
in.
