# Adding, moving and removing children

Most places are decided the normal way: a parent registers, you approve. This
page is for the other cases — a family that wrote to you instead, a child who
is in the wrong class, a place that has to end — and they all happen on the
class's **Roster**.

Open a class and click **Roster**, or click the roster link on the class list.

## Adding a child yourself {#add}

At the top of the roster, under **Add a child**, start typing the child's
name and pick them from the list, then click **Enrol**.

> The child is enrolled **straight away**. There is no request for you to
> approve afterwards, and the family gets the usual "Confirmed" email within
> seconds. So, as with approving: do it once you know the class will run.

What you can and cannot do here:

- The list offers children who already have an account: a parent has added
  them under *My family*. If the family is new to the site, ask them to
  create an account and add their children first — you cannot invent a child
  for them, because the emails have nowhere to go.
- If you look after specific classes rather than all of them, the list shows
  the children of families already in one of your classes. A super admin can
  pick anyone.
- A child who already has a live registration for the class (a request, a
  waiting-list entry, an offer or a place) cannot be added again; the page
  tells you.
- A class that is not open — a draft, a cancelled or archived class, or one
  whose term is over — has no *Add a child* box.

**When the class is full**, the button reads **Enrol (over capacity)** and asks
you to confirm. The child is enrolled anyway and the roster then shows how
far over capacity the class is, exactly as after an over-capacity offer.
This is your call to make; the app only makes sure you make it on purpose.

If the child's age is outside the class's recommended range, you are told
after enrolling — a note, not a refusal.

Nobody ticked the terms and conditions box for a child you add yourself. If
that matters to you, say so to the family.

## Moving a child to another class {#move}

On the roster, every enrolled child and every child on the waiting list has a
**Move…** button. It opens a short page listing the other open classes you
look after, each with its day and time, its provider, and whether it has a
seat free. Pick one and click **Move**.

In one step:

- the old registration ends, marked *Moved to another class* in the child's
  history (so it never reads as a cancellation);
- the child gets a **confirmed place** in the new class — even if they were
  only on the waiting list, or only had a request in, for the old one;
- the family gets **one** email saying where the child now goes, with the new
  day, time and provider. Not a cancellation email followed by a
  confirmation.
- if anyone is waiting for a seat in the old class, you get the usual
  "seat freed" alert.

If the new class is full, the page stops and asks you to confirm, naming the
numbers. **Move anyway (over capacity)** does what it says.

A child who already has a live registration for the class you picked cannot
be moved there; the page tells you and lets you choose again.

You land on the new class's roster, where the child now is.

## Ending a place {#cancel}

Each row on the roster has a button to end that registration, worded for
what it is:

| Row | Button | What the family is told |
|---|---|---|
| Enrolled | **Cancel place** | The school has cancelled the place |
| Outstanding offer | **Withdraw offer** | The school has cancelled it; the seat is free again |
| Waiting list | **Remove** | The school has cancelled the registration |
| Pending request | **Reject** | The request was not approved (see [Approving requests](approving-requests)) |

Each asks you to confirm first, because the email goes out at once and
cannot be recalled.

> A family that has **asked to cancel** themselves shows *Asked to cancel* on
> their row, with **Confirm cancellation** and **Keep place** instead. Use
> those: confirming sends the family the "as you asked" email rather than
> the school's cancellation notice.

A cancelled place frees its seat immediately. If anyone is waiting, the
admins looking after the class get an alert, and [handing out the
seat](waiting-lists#offer) works as usual.

## Undoing a mistake {#undo}

There is no undo button, but nothing is lost:

- **Added the wrong child?** Click **Cancel place** on their row. Two emails
  will have gone to the family, so a word from you is kind.
- **Moved to the wrong class?** Click **Move…** on the new roster and move
  them again, or back.
- **Cancelled by mistake?** Add the child again from **Add a child**. The
  new place is confirmed at once.
