# Your classes and their registers

Click a class on **My classes** to open it. Everything about running that
class is on this one page.

![A class page: instructors, the enrolled children with their going-home arrangement and notes, and the sessions](class-page.png)

## Who teaches it {#instructors}

The instructors assigned to the class are listed first, as parents see them
on the public class page. A provider account changes who is assigned from
the Instructors page ([Managing your instructors](managing-instructors)).

## The register {#register}

**Enrolled children** are the children with a confirmed place. For each one
you see:

- their name and date of birth, and their school class (P3E, S1S...), so you
  can find them at pick-up;
- **Going home** — whether the family has said the child **may leave alone**
  after the class, or **must be collected**. Act on what is written here,
  never on what a child tells you: families change it on their own family
  page, and that is the record;
- **Notes for you** — what the family wants you to know: allergies, medical
  needs, anything that helps. Read them before the first session;
- the guardians' names, in case you need to reach a family in a hurry.

> This is children's personal information. Use it for running the class and
> nothing else, and do not copy it elsewhere.

The **waiting list** below shows who is hoping for a place, in order. You
cannot give places out; the school office does that, and a child moves from
the waiting list to the register when the office confirms them.

Children whose request the office has not yet reviewed are not shown at all.

## Sessions {#sessions}

The **Sessions** table is the class calendar for the term: one row per
lesson, with school holidays already skipped (they are listed above the
table). Each future lesson is **Planned**; a lesson the office has called off
is **Cancelled**, with its note beside it, and the families have been told.

The **Attendance** link on each row is where you mark who came
([Taking attendance](attendance)). The **Take attendance** button at the top
of the page goes straight to the next lesson.

## Something wrong? {#office}

A child missing from the register, a wrong time, a session that should not
be there: tell the school office. The dashboard shows what the office has
set up, and only they can change it.
