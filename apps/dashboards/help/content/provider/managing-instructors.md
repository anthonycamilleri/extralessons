# Managing your instructors

*For provider accounts. If you are an instructor, your classes and your
profile are in the other guides.*

The **Instructors** page lists the people who teach for you, with their
classes, the state of their certificate of police conduct and whether they
have logged in yet.

![The Instructors page: each instructor with their classes, certificate and account status](instructors.png)

## Adding an instructor {#add}

Press **Add an instructor**. Enter their email address and name, tick the
classes they will teach, and save.

![The add-an-instructor form](add-instructor.png)

What happens next depends on the address:

- **A new address**: an account is created and the instructor is emailed a
  link to choose a password. The link works for three days. Their status
  shows **Invitation sent** until they log in.
- **The address of an instructor who already works for another provider**:
  their existing account is linked to you as well. They get no email; tell
  them yourself.
- **A parent's or the school's address**: refused. An account has one role,
  so an instructor who is also a parent at the school needs a separate email
  address for teaching.

Once they are in, they see the classes you ticked, their registers and
attendance, and they can message those families. Ask them to fill in their
profile and upload their certificate ([Your profile and certificate of police
conduct](your-profile)); the Instructors page shows you when they have.

## If you teach too {#self}

Press **I teach too** at the top of your provider's panel. Your own account
gets an instructor profile: parents see you on your class pages, and you can
upload your own certificate. You keep seeing every class; the profile is
added, nothing is taken away.

## An instructor's page {#detail}

Click a name to open the instructor's page: their profile as parents see it,
their login and whether they have used it, their certificate and a
**Download** link, and the classes they teach.

![An instructor's page: profile, certificate, and the classes they teach](instructor-page.png)

- **Classes**: tick and untick, then **Save classes**. It takes effect at
  once; a class they are taken off disappears from their dashboard.
- **Send the invitation again**, shown while they have not logged in, sends a
  fresh set-your-password link. Once they have logged in, the login page's
  **Forgotten your password?** is the way back in.
- **Edit profile** lets you fill in or correct their profile on their behalf,
  or upload a certificate they sent to you.

## When someone leaves {#remove}

**Remove instructor** on their page takes them off your provider: their
profile, their certificate and their class assignments are deleted, and
their account is switched off unless another provider still uses it. Their
name stays on the attendance they recorded.

There is no undo; adding them again creates a fresh profile.

## What the school sees {#school}

The school office sees every instructor in its admin: their profile, their
classes, and the state of their certificate. Marking a certificate as checked
is the office's job, and the badge parents see follows from it. If an
instructor's certificate has been uploaded for a while and is still not
marked checked, ask the office.
