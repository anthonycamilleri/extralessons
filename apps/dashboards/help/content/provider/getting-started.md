# Getting started

The provider dashboard is where you run your classes at the school: who is
in them, who turned up, and a way to write to their families. This page is
the tour; the other guides go into each part.

## Logging in {#login}

Your login is your email address. If the school office or your provider set
up your account, you were emailed a link to choose a password. If that link
has expired, or you never had one, use **Forgotten your password?** on the
login page and a new link is sent to you. Once you are in, **Password** in
the top bar changes it at any time.

After logging in you land on **My classes**.

## Two kinds of account {#accounts}

Everyone on the provider side has one of two kinds of account, and some
people have both.

- A **provider account** belongs to the organisation: the academy, the
  company, the sole trader. It sees every class the school has set up under
  that provider, can write to all of their families, and manages the
  **instructors** — the people who actually take the classes. If you run the
  provider, this is you.
- An **instructor** account belongs to one person who teaches. It sees only
  the classes assigned to them, and has a profile that parents read on the
  class page.

If you both run the provider and teach, you use one login for both: your
provider account also gets an instructor profile when you press **I teach
too** on the Instructors page (see [Managing your instructors](managing-instructors)).

## What is on the dashboard {#dashboard}

![My classes: every class you work with, with its term, schedule, instructors and numbers](dashboard.png)

**My classes** lists every class you work with: its term, day and time, who
teaches it, how many children are enrolled against the number of places, and
how many are waiting. Click a class for its register and sessions
([Your classes and their registers](your-classes)).

Along the top:

- **Message families** writes to the families of your classes
  ([Messaging families](messaging-families)).
- **Instructors** — provider accounts only — manages the people who teach for
  you ([Managing your instructors](managing-instructors)).
- **My profile** — instructors only — is what parents read about you, and
  where your certificate of police conduct goes
  ([Your profile and certificate of police conduct](your-profile)).

If your profile is incomplete, a note at the top of the page says what is
missing until it is done.

## What you cannot do here {#office}

The school office sets up the classes: their times, places, prices and
capacity, which term they run in, and who gets a place. If a class of yours
is missing, a time is wrong, or a child should be moved, tell the office and
they change it; you will see the change straight away.
