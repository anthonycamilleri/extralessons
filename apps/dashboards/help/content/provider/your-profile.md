# Your profile and certificate of police conduct

Every instructor has a short profile. Parents read it on the page of every
class you teach, under **Who runs it**, and the school office sees it in the
admin. Open **My profile** in the top bar to fill it in.

![The profile form: name, a few lines about you, a photo, and the certificate](profile-form.png)

## What parents see {#public}

- Your **name**, as you enter it here.
- **About you**: a few lines on what you teach, your background, and what a
  session with you is like. Two or three sentences are plenty. Write it for
  a parent deciding whether to sign their child up.
- An optional **photo**. It is shrunk to a small square, so a clear picture of
  your face works best.
- Whether the school has **checked your certificate of police conduct**. The
  badge appears only after the office has looked at your certificate; until
  then parents see your name and text with no badge.

If you teach for more than one provider you have one profile per provider,
and **My profile** lets you choose which to edit.

## The certificate of police conduct {#certificate}

The school asks every instructor who works with its children for a current
certificate of police conduct (conduct certificate). Upload it here as a PDF
or a clear photo, JPG or PNG, up to 10 MB, and enter the date it was issued.

> **Who can see it.** The file is private. Only you, your provider's account
> and the school office can open it; it is never shown to parents and never
> appears on a public page. Parents see one thing only: that the school has
> checked it.

Once it is uploaded, the page shows **Uploaded, awaiting the school's check**.
The office opens the document, satisfies itself, and marks it checked; the
page then shows **Checked by the school** with the date, and the badge appears
on your class pages.

When your certificate is renewed, upload the new one in the same place. It
replaces the old file, and the check starts again, because it is a new
document.

## If you do not have one yet {#none}

Fill in the rest of your profile anyway; you can add the certificate later.
Your provider and the school office can see on their side that it is still
missing, and will remind you. Until it is checked, your profile simply has no
badge.

## Your provider can help {#provider}

Your provider's account can open your profile too, to fix a typo or upload a
certificate you sent them. Your login and password are yours alone.
