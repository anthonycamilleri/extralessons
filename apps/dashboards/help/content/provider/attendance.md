# Taking attendance

Every lesson has its own attendance page, and the school keeps what you
record there. Families can see their own child's attendance on their family
page, so it is worth getting right.

## Marking a lesson {#mark}

On the class page, press **Take attendance** at the top (that is the next
lesson) or the **Attendance** link on any row of the sessions table.

![The attendance page: one row per enrolled child, ticked when present](attendance.png)

Every enrolled child is listed and **ticked**. Untick the ones who were not
there, then press **Save attendance**. The care notes are shown beside each
name so you have them in front of you while you take the register.

That is all there is to it. You can take attendance before the lesson, during
it on a phone, or afterwards.

## Correcting it {#edit}

Open the same lesson again: the page tells you attendance was already taken
and shows the ticks as you left them. Change them and save. The last save
counts.

## Cancelled lessons {#cancelled}

A lesson the office has cancelled has no attendance link; there was nothing
to attend. If a lesson did not take place and is not marked cancelled, tell
the office rather than marking everyone absent, so the families are told the
right thing.

## Who is on the list {#who}

Only children with a confirmed place are listed. A child who turned up
without being on the register is not enrolled: let the office know, and do
not mark them under someone else's name.
