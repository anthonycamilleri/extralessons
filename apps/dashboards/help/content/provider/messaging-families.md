# Messaging families

**Message families** on your dashboard sends one email (and a WhatsApp
message, for families who asked for it) to the families of the classes you
choose. Use it for a change of room, a kit reminder, a lesson that will end
early.

![The message form: which classes, a subject and the message](message-form.png)

## Writing it {#write}

Tick the **classes** the message is for. Only classes you work with are
listed. Give it a **subject**, which becomes the email subject line, and
write the **message** as you would to a parent. Each family sees it opening
with their own name and signed off by the school, so start with what you
want to say.

The toolbar gives you bold, italic, headings, lists, links and pictures. To
add a picture, press the picture button, or paste or drag one into the
message. The email keeps your formatting and pictures; the WhatsApp version
carries the words only, so never let a picture carry the whole point.

## Who gets it {#who}

> The message goes to **every guardian of every child with a live place** in
> the classes you tick — and that includes children on the waiting list and
> children whose place the office has not yet confirmed, not only the ones on
> your register.

That is usually right: a cancelled first lesson concerns everyone who thinks
they might be coming. If a message only makes sense for the children who are
actually enrolled, say so in the message.

Nobody is emailed twice: a parent with two children in two of your classes
gets one email.

## Check it before it goes {#test}

Press **Send me a test email** under the message first. It arrives in your
own inbox exactly as a family will see it. Read it on your phone, fix what
you want to fix, and send yourself another test if you like; nothing is
recorded.

Then press **Send message**. You are told how many families it reached.

> There is no draft and no unsend. The test email is your preview.

## Replies {#replies}

Replies go to the school's address, not to you, so the office sees them. If
you want families to be able to reach you directly, say how in the message.
